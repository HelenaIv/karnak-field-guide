# Media acquisition and commercial reuse

Commercial reuse is preferred regardless of whether the app is ultimately paid or free. Free distribution does not remove copyright obligations.

Store each selected file's creator, original title, file-description URL, original download URL, licence/version/link, attribution wording, modifications, retrieval date, local path, byte count, SHA-256 and supported subject IDs in `content/media.json`. Preserve source metadata under `research/media-provenance/`. Visual identification and licence eligibility are different checks.

The first three original photographs are by Diego Delso, obtained from Wikimedia Commons; the image metadata identifies CC BY-SA 4.0. This licence permits commercial sharing and adaptation with conditions. Credit the creator and source, link the licence, identify modifications, license adaptations appropriately and do not impose incompatible additional restrictions on these assets. It does not require indiscriminately licensing all independent app code under the image licence.

The photographer's credit page requests legible adjacent attribution: “Diego Delso, delso.photo, License CC BY-SA”. Retain those linked credits next to each photo and in offline credits. Notification is requested by the creator; no message has been sent. No contact is necessary merely to acquire these files under their stated licence.

Original masters are preserved unchanged; smaller pack derivatives are still needed. Record crops, colour changes, labels or conversions. Don't label a research photograph as a precise scene-identification aid until it has been matched to the edition. Don't hotlink the production app to Commons.

Store packaging must preserve licence freedoms for these images; review DRM/terms and provide an accessible openly licensed copy if needed. If a particular image cannot be distributed compatibly, select CC0/public-domain/CC BY material or obtain separate permission. The media registry is an evidence trail, not a blanket warranty covering all possible non-copyright rights.

Academic site availability is not a reuse licence. OIP 142's catalogue currently displays CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; its illustrations are not imported for a commercial app on that basis. Other academic imagery remains reference-only until its own rights are established.

Sources checked 2026-11-13: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [photographer's credit instructions](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Poco_a_poco/credit), individual Commons file pages and API metadata in the registry. Offline source/creator/licence URLs and attribution must ship with the pack, not rely on a network credits page.
