# 2026-11-21

## Home page: general first screen and a quiz

Owner feedback: the home page felt like a Hall guide (Hall photo, "Start the Hall guide" button). She wanted the first screen to show all main sections, and something engaging such as a quiz.

Done:
- Hero: kept the eyebrow, title and intro text; removed the Hall photo, its credit, the "A closer look / editorial draft" badge and the "Start the Hall guide" button. The right half is now a dark panel "Inside the guide" listing all eight sections in menu order plus Saved (number, title, one-line description, arrow). On phones it becomes a two-column grid of titles, and the meta line is hidden, so all sections show on the first screen (checked at 1280×800 and 390×844 in en, uk, nb; at 320×640 the last row is just below the fold).
- Removed the separate "Where to start." card block added on 2026-11-20 (the hero panel replaces it). Its uk/nb strings stay in ui.json, unused.
- New "Test your eye." quiz under the hero: five multiple-choice questions, one at a time, with right/wrong marking, a one-line explanation, a "Read more →" link to the entry the answer comes from, and a score at the end. Questions and answers are taken from existing entries: a-roof-you-can-no-longer-see (134 columns = 12 + 122; the roof has fallen), raised-sunk-recut (sunk relief), chapels-with-second-lives (White Chapel blocks reused in Amenhotep III's Third Pylon), akhmenu-tent-columns (tent-pole columns). Code: `QUIZ`, `quizSection`, `quizInteract` in `web/app.js`; styles `.hero-index`, `.hi-*`, `.qz-*` in `web/styles.css`.
- uk and nb strings for the panel and all quiz text. Ukrainian uses "врізаний рельєф" as elsewhere in the uk content; Norwegian uses Thutmose. Norwegian is still unreviewed machine translation.

Limits:
- Quiz has a fixed set of five questions; no shuffling, no saved score.
- Not checked on real phones.

## Home page with photos, Egyptian-styled section panel; area links fixed

Owner feedback: the home page had no images and needed to "wow" new visitors; the dark green section panel was boring and should feel Egyptian; and clicking an area (e.g. the precinct of Mut) on the home page led to a map where nothing told you what to do.

Done:
- Hero: full-width slideshow of six photos (First Pylon and sphinx avenue, Sacred Lake, owner's obelisk view, Great Court sphinxes, owner's painted columns, aerial view), changing every 6.5 s with a slow zoom (off with reduced motion), dark gradient behind the title, caption and credit of the current photo, dots to pick a photo. Slideshow stops when you leave the home page.
- Section panel: translucent dark panel over the photo with a painted-cornice frieze (blue, red, green blocks between gold lines) at top and bottom, and a gold line icon per section drawn for the guide in the manner of hieroglyphs: pylon (Explore), cartouche (Read the walls), winged sun (The Context), papyrus column (Hall guide), papyrus scroll (Glossary), eye (Image collection), lotus (Misc), ankh (Saved). Phones: two-column tiles with icons.
- Quiz: a photo for each question, with its credit.
- "A temple of many worlds": every area card shows a photo of one of its places; photo credits listed under the grid.
- Explore map: arriving from an area link scrolls to the map; the card under the map names the area and lists its places as buttons; an area with one place opens it directly (Mut, Northern temples, Sacred Lake, temple core); the precinct of Mut, which is not drawn on the map, shows its location text ("Outside this map. ..."). The hint and location note show on phones too (the card hides its paragraphs there).
- uk and nb strings for the new text. "Photo: Helena Ivanova" keeps the name unchanged in both.

Checked: Chrome at 1280, 1000, 390 and 320 px in en, uk and nb; no sideways scrolling; all section rows on the first screen at 1280×800 and 390×844; area links tested for five areas.

Limits: not checked on real phones; the slideshow adds about 2 MB to the first home page load online (all photos were already in the offline pack).
