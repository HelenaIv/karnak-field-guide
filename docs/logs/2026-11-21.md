## Home page with photos, Egyptian-styled section panel; area links fixed

Owner feedback: the home page had no images and needed to "wow" new visitors; the dark green section panel was boring and should feel Egyptian; and clicking an area (e.g. the precinct of Mut) on the home page led to a map where nothing told you what to do.

Done:
- Hero: full-width slideshow of six photos (First Pylon and sphinx avenue, Sacred Lake, owner's obelisk view, Great Court sphinxes, owner's painted columns, aerial view), changing every 6.5 s with a slow zoom (off with reduced motion), dark gradient behind the title, caption and credit of the current photo, dots to pick a photo. Slideshow stops when you leave the home page.
- Section panel: translucent dark panel over the photo with a painted-cornice frieze (blue, red, green blocks between gold lines) at top and bottom, and a gold line icon per section drawn for the guide in the manner of hieroglyphs: pylon (Explore), cartouche (Read the walls), winged sun (The Context), papyrus column (Hall guide), papyrus scroll (Glossary), eye (Image collection), lotus (Misc), ankh (Saved). Phones: two-column tiles with icons.
- Quiz: a photo for each question, with its credit.
- "A temple of many worlds": every area card shows a photo of one of its places; photo credits listed under the grid.
- Explore map: arriving from an area link scrolls to the map; the card under the map names the area and lists its places as buttons; an area with one place opens it directly (Mut, Northern temples, Sacred Lake, temple core); the precinct of Mut, which is not drawn on the map, shows its location text ("Outside this map. ..."). The hint and location note show on phones too (the card hides its paragraphs there).
- uk and nb strings for the new text. "Photo: Helena Ivanova" keeps the name unchanged in both.

Checked: Chrome at 1280, 1000, 390 and 320 px in en, uk and nb; no sideways scrolling; all section rows on the first screen at 1280×800 and 390×844; area links tested for five areas.

Limits: not checked on real phones; the slideshow adds about 2 MB to the first home page load online (all photos were already in the offline pack).

## New Context page: Main ideas of Egyptian religion (`#basics/religion`)

Owner request: collect content and add a page on the main ideas of ancient Egyptian religion, very visual, schematic and interactive, as a subsection of The Context; deep and engaging, not popular legends.

Sources (new in `content/sources.json`, all read 2026-11-21 in the browser): UCL Digital Egypt "Gods and goddesses: creation" (`ucl-deities-creation`), "Gods and goddesses in ancient Egyptian belief" (`ucl-deities-index`), "Kingship: what does a king have to do?" (`ucl-king-duties`); The Met, Divine Egypt gallery texts (`met-divine-egypt`); British Museum, Book of the Dead of Hunefer EA9901,3 (`bm-hunefer`); Fitzwilliam Museum, "The soul in ancient Egypt" (`fitz-soul`). Also existing Memphis Hypostyle pages on ritual scenes and `hall-amun`. Not usable: UCLA Encyclopedia of Egyptology on eScholarship and Smarthistory (bot checks), so the UEE articles on akh, ba and ethics were not used.

Page: seven ideas, each with an original interactive drawing, a "Look closer" note with the scholarly caveats, an "At Karnak" pointer where supported, and its own sources:
1. Maat: the king's six duties from the New Kingdom text, around the feather (tap a duty); note on what the list hides (army, economy).
2. Gods: six heads (falcon, lioness, cow horns, ibis, jackal, human) with what the head signals, photos where the library has one; Amun + Ra join into Amun-Ra (animated); names as areas of divine power, three ways of grouping gods.
3. Creation: the Eight as four absences (tap to reveal), mound / heron / lotus scene, family tree Ra-Atum to Horus (tap a god to trace the line), the Heavenly Cow story, people from the sun god's tears; caveat that "Hermopolitan/Heliopolitan theology" are modern labels and the material is not one story.
4. The sun: day and night circle with the boat moving through four phases, play button, snake in the underworld at night.
5. Temple exchange: tap a royal gift, a chip travels to the god and a divine gift comes back; how to read a scene; how non-royal people reached the gods.
6. The person: body, ka, ba, shadow, heart, akh on one drawing (the heart drawn as the Egyptian vessel, not a modern heart shape).
7. Judgement: Hunefer's scene in six steps; the balance levels at the weighing step.

Also: hub of the seven ideas at the top; card on the Context home (photo: own-relief-king-bouquet-falcon-god) and links in the connection map (group "What it meant"). Ukrainian: all page content and source descriptions (100% coverage); interface strings in uk and nb. `scripts/i18n.py` KEYS extended with the new field names. Context map: topic buttons no longer overflow at 320 px (an older problem, worse with three topics in one group).

Checked: Chrome at 1280, 390 and 320 px in en, uk, nb; every interactive part clicked; no errors; no sideways scrolling on the page or on the Context home.

Limits: Norwegian page content stays English (as for all content); the judgement drawing is small on 320 px phones; figures are schematic, not copies of the papyrus; not checked on real phones; app not rebuilt.
