# 2026-11-21

## Home page: general first screen and a quiz

Owner feedback: the home page felt like a Hall guide (Hall photo, "Start the Hall guide" button). She wanted the first screen to show all main sections, and something engaging such as a quiz.

Done:
- Hero: kept the eyebrow, title and intro text; removed the Hall photo, its credit, the "A closer look / editorial draft" badge and the "Start the Hall guide" button. The right half is now a dark panel "Inside the guide" listing all eight sections in menu order plus Saved (number, title, one-line description, arrow). On phones it becomes a two-column grid of titles, and the meta line is hidden, so all sections show on the first screen (checked at 1280×800 and 390×844 in en, uk, nb; at 320×640 the last row is just below the fold).
- Removed the separate "Where to start." card block added on 2026-11-20 (the hero panel replaces it). Its uk/nb strings stay in ui.json, unused.
- New "Test your eye." quiz under the hero: five multiple-choice questions, one at a time, with right/wrong marking, a one-line explanation, a "Read more →" link to the entry the answer comes from, and a score at the end. Questions and answers are taken from existing entries: a-roof-you-can-no-longer-see (134 columns = 12 + 122; the roof has fallen), raised-sunk-recut (sunk relief), chapels-with-second-lives (White Chapel blocks reused in Amenhotep III's Third Pylon), akhmenu-tent-columns (tent-pole columns). Code: `QUIZ`, `quizSection`, `quizInteract` in `web/app.js`; styles `.hero-index`, `.hi-*`, `.qz-*` in `web/styles.css`.
- uk and nb strings for the panel and all quiz text. Ukrainian uses "врізаний рельєф" as elsewhere in the uk content; Norwegian uses Thutmose. Norwegian is still unreviewed machine translation.

Limits:
- Quiz has a fixed set of five questions; no shuffling, no saved score.
- Not checked on real phones.
