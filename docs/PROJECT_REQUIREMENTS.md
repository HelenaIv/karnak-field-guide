# Confirmed project requirements

Recovered and confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-13. This file replaces dependence on the lost initial chats. Do not claim to have recovered information that was not repeated here.

## Product and editorial direction

- A historically accurate, location-based Karnak Temple field guide.
- The distinctive value is close observation: small architectural features, meaningful signs, reading walls, carving techniques, alterations, light, construction and religious context. A generic highlights walk is insufficient.
- Cover monuments, historical periods, pharaohs, architecture, inscriptions, religion, routes and relationships. Monument overviews support deeper detail entries.
- The assistant is responsible for research and gathering content using reliable archaeological and Egyptological sources, with citations and explicit uncertainty.
- English first; Ukrainian planned. Use locale code `uk`, not `ua`.
- Text, images and selected animations. No audio guide.

## Technology and distribution

- WordPress with JetEngine is the backend. The owner has no WordPress site yet and can create one when needed.
- A separate app sits on top of the backend and should open across devices.
- Users should be able to download the guide and access its information with minimal internet dependence.
- Preserve a path to commercial distribution through Google Play and Apple's App Store. Store publication has not been requested yet.
- Prefer imagery eligible for commercial reuse; record evidence of eligibility and fulfil the licence conditions. A free release is an option if necessary, not a confirmed business model.
